03 Experience

From full-stack to research to production AI

  1. 2024 — Now

    AI Engineer · Agile Actors

    • Build and ship a production multi-agent assistant that turns natural-language questions into SQL queries over a relational database.
    • Secure it with guardrails, including SQL validation, prompt hardening and attack monitoring.
    • Track SQL-generation accuracy with an LLM-as-judge evaluation pipeline I designed, and benchmark LLM systems for accuracy and faithfulness before they ship.
    • Architect multi-agent chatbots and stateful LLM workflows with LangChain and LangGraph.
    • Build agentic RAG pipelines for complex document and image retrieval across multiple databases.
    • Turn unstructured Word documents into structured Markdown with a pipeline built on Azure Document Intelligence.
    • Deploy and operate containerised services on OpenShift, including incident diagnosis and resolution.

  2. 2023 — 2025

    NLP Researcher · AUEB Natural Language Processing Group

    • Research diagnostic captioning with multimodal LLMs, using prompt engineering and retrieval augmentation to improve caption quality.
    • First author of the ImageCLEFmedical 2024 submission (2nd in Concept Detection).

  3. 2022 — 2023

    Junior Full Stack Engineer · HOMI Greece

    • Built classification and regression models for predictive tasks and deployed them on AWS SageMaker.
    • Shipped FastAPI services and React interfaces on top of PostgreSQL and MongoDB.
